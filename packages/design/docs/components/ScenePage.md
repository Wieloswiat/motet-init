# ScenePage

One Scene page assembled from the system, switchable between ten Worlds and three reader modes. Elarid, Oskad and Mgławica Mocy follow real play-by-forum games (two Wieloświat categories and the Mgławica Mocy archive): their settings and section names, with invented characters and original art.

Use it as the reference composition: chrome islands over the World's art, the content sheet rising over it with the World's edge, the World identity and tabs, the scene heading with fields and the lane switch, the post stream with a roll event between two posts, an OOC post, the composer, and the cast rail on wide screens. The header and the background are one image: Salt Road, Ashfall and The Gilded Reach use `continuous`, Neon Meridian, Hollowmere and Kepler Relay `backdrop`, Cold Front `header`. The sheet's sides follow its top: Salt Road's fade dissolves into the art, harder edges get straight sides. Kepler Relay (veil) and Neon Meridian (frosted) use translucent sheets. Widen the window past 1200px to see the art beside the sheet.

The small island at the bottom right exists only in this preview, to switch the sample World and the reader mode. "World default" uses each World's preferred mode; Light and Dark are explicit reader choices, which win.

The art is procedural placeholder material generated in `components/demo/worlds.js`.
