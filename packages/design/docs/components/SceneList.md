# SceneList

Scenes grouped by Arc (Story lens) or Location (Places lens), with unread, your turn and draft as separate signals.

Rows: unread bar, title, location and arc fields, flags and a plain unread count, the cast, and relative time. The cast and time are fixed-width columns at the end, so rows line up however many signals each carries. The cast is least important and hides first, below 640px; below 460px flags move under the title. Reading never clears "Your turn".
