# Chrome

Motet's corner islands: Home and World switcher on the left; Search, Inbox and Account on the right.

**Use** on every page, World or global. **Avoid** adding World navigation or actions to the islands.

- Material: veil by default, solid under reduced transparency, frosted for comparison (see the Chrome section).
- Inside an island all World variables are reset to Motet's; a World may tint the fill up to 8%.
- The World switcher shows only the mark; the menu lists your Worlds with their marks and states ("Your turn", "4 new").
- The account menu always offers appearance switches and "Use Motet appearance here".
- Accessibility: the left island is a `nav` labelled Motet; the right is a `toolbar`. The switcher's accessible name includes the current World. Counts are in the Inbox button's name, not only in the bubble.
